Spark Resume Screener

Distributed pipeline that classifies resumes into 43 job categories: Spark OCR (Tess4J), Spark NLP NER with ESCO skill matching, then a fine-tuned RoBERTa model, served through a Flask app that scores job fit.

  • 81.5% test accuracy, weighted F1 0.814
